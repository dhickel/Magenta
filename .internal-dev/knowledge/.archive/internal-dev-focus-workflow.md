# Internal Dev Focus Workflow

## Topic

Strict-schema living focus records for `.internal-dev`.

## Source References

- `.internal-dev/focus/AGENTS.md`
- `.internal-dev/AGENTS.md`
- `AGENTS.md`

## Key Takeaways

- `focus/` is the running operating picture and does not replace plans, bugs, changelogs, reviews, notes, or knowledge.
- `current-focus.md` tracks long-term direction, not a short sprint or task.
- `unfinished-work.md` is the required place to record work left incomplete, blocked, paused, handed off, resumed, or closed.
- `ideas-inbox.md` is raw intake; `horizon-ideas.md` is curated future work.
- `decisions.md` should link to `.internal-dev/knowledge/` when a decision has reusable lessons.
- Agents should report stale strategic focus during closeout instead of silently rewriting the project direction.

## Engine Relevance

The workflow gives agents a small, schema-stable context surface for project direction and handoff state. It reduces broad `.internal-dev` scans while making closeout automation more reliable.
